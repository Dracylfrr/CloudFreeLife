# Roadmap

Plans, not promises. Cloud-Freelife stays small, calm and offline-first.

## 1.0.1 — polish
- Signed installers (Authenticode) to avoid SmartScreen warnings.
- Automatic installer launch after a verified update download (still user-initiated).
- Pressure-sensitive pens and touch pinch-zoom.
- Brush cursor preview that matches texture brushes more closely.
- More automated UI tests (Playwright against the Tauri window).

## 1.1.0 — shipped
Castle Dreams, Ocean Friends, Memory Meadow, *Castle Twilight* and *Ocean Pearl* palettes. See the changelog.

## 1.1.1 — shipped
Sweet Treats, Forest Friends, Pastel Puzzle, mini-game cards and the friendlier update notice.

## 1.2.0 — shipped
Cozy Stories, Story Adventures, eight new coloring pages and the Sleepy Bear fix.

## 1.3.0 — shipped
Illustrated Cozy Stories and four new stories, Read Aloud (removed again in 1.4.0), the Reading List (Continue / Not started / Finished / Favorites), four new Story Adventures, eight themes plus Follow System, mood presets, generated background music and redesigned soft sounds, an Update Center that stays inside the app, three new coloring books (Mountain Village, Night Garden, Happy Farm) and five palettes (*Candle Glow*, *Blossom Rain*, *Mountain Morning*, *Night Garden*, *Farm Sunrise*).

## 1.4.0 — shipped (a larger, peaceful world)
**Meditation** (six optional quiet sessions and a Silent Timer; no streaks, no failure state, no medical claims), **Gentle Movement** (ten stretch cards), four new relaxing mini-games (**Bubble Garden**, **Cloud Catcher**, **Zen Garden**, **Paint Flow**), six more levels in each older mini-game, Splash Wall saving to the Gallery, a choice of five app icons, Home cards for Meditation and Gentle Movement, and Cozy Stories and Story Adventures as two separate places again.

## 1.5.0 — long reads
- Very long stories and adventures (30 minutes to several hours) with chapters and bookmarks.
- More meditation and story packs delivered as content packs.

## Later ideas
- Installable themes as content packs.
- Monetization and licensing architecture (to be evaluated separately; nothing is built for it).
- Layers for Free Draw.
- Import your own line art (SVG or PNG) as a coloring page.
- Timelapse export.
- macOS and Linux builds (the code is already cross-platform; Windows is what is tested and shipped).
