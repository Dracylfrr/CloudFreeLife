# Creating Meditation sessions and Exercise cards

Both are plain JSON plus one small cover picture, like every other kind of content. Put them under `content/` to bundle them, or in a content pack to ship them later ([PUBLISHING_CONTENT.md](PUBLISHING_CONTENT.md), kinds `meditation` and `movement`).

## The tone to keep

These screens exist for a quiet moment, so the writing matters as much as the data:

- **Optional and kind.** Nothing to achieve, nothing to fail. No streaks, goals, scores, levels, rewards or "you missed a day". Ending early is always fine.
- **No medical claims.** Do not promise to treat, cure, prevent or relieve anything (no "reduces anxiety", "lowers blood pressure", "fixes back pain"). Say what the exercise is, and invite, don't instruct: "you can", "if it feels comfortable".
- **No pressure words.** Avoid *must*, *should*, *perfect* and anything about winning or losing. The test suite (`tests/wellness.test.ts`) scans the bundled text and fails on *streak*, *score*, *rank*, *leaderboard*, *fail / failed / failure*, *guilt*, *calories*, *burn* and *weight loss*.
- **Short sentences.** A cue is read for a few seconds on screen. Keep it under 140 characters.

## Meditation sessions

One folder per session: `content/meditation/<id>/` containing `session.json` and a cover (`cover.svg`, 4:3 works well).

```json
{
  "id": "cloud_breathing",
  "name": "Cloud Breathing",
  "description": "Breathe along with a soft little cloud that grows and settles. About three minutes.",
  "accent": "#a698e0",
  "kind": "breathing",
  "minutes": 3,
  "breath": { "inhale": 4, "hold": 2, "exhale": 6, "rest": 1 },
  "ambience": "hush",
  "scene": "cloud",
  "cues": [
    { "at": 0, "text": "Imagine a small, soft cloud in front of you." },
    { "at": 25, "text": "As you breathe in, it grows. As you breathe out, it softens." }
  ],
  "order": 2,
  "version": "1.4.0",
  "minAppVersion": "1.4.0",
  "cover": "cover.svg"
}
```

- `kind`: `breathing` (the cloud follows a breathing pattern; needs `breath`), `stillness` (a quiet scene with cues, no breathing guide) or `timer` (a silent timer; needs `durations`).
- `minutes`: 1 to 60. For a `timer` it is the default length.
- `durations` (timer only): up to eight lengths in minutes the person can choose from, for example `[1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20]`.
- `breath`: seconds for `inhale`, `hold`, `exhale` and `rest`. Inhale and exhale are at least 2; hold and rest may be 0. A slow, even pattern (4-2-6-1) is a good default.
- `cues`: short lines shown at `at` seconds from the start (0 to 3600). Up to 80. Missing or bad cues are dropped, the session still runs.
- `ambience`: `none`, `hush` (just the soft room), `rain` (soft generated rain) or `garden` (the Quiet Garden music track). It only plays while the session runs and the person can mute it with the *Sound* button.
- `scene`: the picture behind the cloud: `cloud`, `garden`, `rain`, `evening` or `dots`.
- `accent`: the session's color (`#rrggbb`).
- `order`: where it sits in the list (small numbers first).

The app remembers only the last session, your favorites and how many times each was finished, in `progress\meditation.json`. A session you add later simply shows up with no history.

## Exercise cards

One folder per card: `content/movement/<id>/` containing `card.json` and `cover.svg`.

```json
{
  "id": "ankle_circles",
  "name": "Ankle Circles",
  "description": "Slow ankle circles to wake up tired feet.",
  "minutes": 2,
  "position": "Seated",
  "accent": "#8ed2b2",
  "steps": [
    { "title": "Lift one foot", "text": "Sit tall and lift one foot a little off the floor." },
    { "title": "Trace a circle", "text": "Draw slow, smooth circles with your toes, five in each direction." }
  ],
  "order": 8,
  "version": "1.4.0",
  "minAppVersion": "1.4.0",
  "cover": "cover.svg"
}
```

- `steps`: 1 to 12 steps, each with a `title` and a short `text`. The card shows one step at a time with Previous and Next. End with a "rest and notice" step that mentions going slowly and stopping if anything hurts.
- `minutes`: 1 to 30, shown as "About N minutes" and used by the optional timer.
- `position`: a short label such as `Seated`, `Standing` or `Seated or standing`.
- The screen always shows: *General movement ideas, not medical advice. Go slowly, stay comfortable and stop if anything hurts.*

### Illustrations

Covers are small SVGs drawn in the app's soft style. The bundled ones are generated by `scripts/wellness/meditation.mjs` and `scripts/wellness/movement.mjs` (a rounded character called Puff, drawn from a few joint angles), so new poses are a few lines of data. You can also hand-draw an SVG or use a PNG. Do not use `<script>`, external links or embedded images in an SVG.

## Checking your work

```powershell
npm test      # validates every bundled session and card and checks the wording
npm run dev   # open Meditation / Exercise and try it
```

A session or card with a missing required field is skipped and never breaks the app.
